Parallax — Content Disclaimer

Last updated 16 September 2026.

Parallax publishes geopolitical analysis and opinion. Please read this before relying on anything in the app.

Forecasts are opinions, and they will sometimes be wrong.

The probabilities, risk levels, and predictions in Parallax are subjective analytical estimates, not facts and not guarantees. A number like "60%" means the desk judges something roughly three-in-five likely — it does not mean it will happen, and by design many such forecasts resolve the other way. The app publishes its own scored track record, including its misses, precisely because it expects to be wrong a meaningful share of the time.

Statements about people and organizations are commentary.

Where the app discusses governments, institutions, officials, or public figures — including sections that examine competing explanations, actors' interests, and claims circulating publicly — those are opinions and interpretations drawn from public reporting and primary documents, offered for analysis and debate. They are not assertions of fact about anyone and should not be taken as such. Where a claim is contested, the app aims to say so and to attribute documents and policies to their named authors and institutions.

This is not advice.

Parallax is not financial, investment, trading, legal, security, safety, or travel advice, and it is not a recommendation to take or avoid any action. Do not make trading, financial, safety, travel, or emergency decisions based on this app. Consult a qualified professional for any real decision.

Sources.

Analysis is synthesized from public news reporting, primary documents, public statistical sources (such as the World Bank, IMF, UN Comtrade, UCDP, ACLED, and the CIA World Factbook), and academic and think-tank work. The desk aims to characterize others' work fairly and to distinguish established fact, reported claim, and its own inference — but makes no warranty of accuracy or completeness.
