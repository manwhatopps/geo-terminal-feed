Parallax

A geopolitical intelligence desk in your pocket. It reads the wires, the primary documents and the public data, and files one deep, sourced brief on every story that matters — then says what it thinks will happen, as a number, and keeps score.

What is in every story

  • The facts on the recordEvery established fact, numbered, each with its source and time — and disputed facts carried in both versions.
  • Who decides, and what they can actually doThe institutions acting, not the podiums narrating afterwards.
  • In their shoesEach principal's position reasoned from the inside: what they need, what they fear, what they cannot afford, and their best move.
  • The numbersTrade, output, military spending, conflict intensity and refugee counts from the World Bank, IMF, UN Comtrade, UCDP and ACLED — each with its vintage.
  • The desk's callA dated probability with the case for and against it, the precedents behind it, and what would change the desk's mind.
  • What the boards sayClaims circulating publicly, decomposed into checkable pieces and graded one by one against the documented record.

How it keeps itself honest

Every forecast is written down, dated, and scored when it resolves — hits and misses both, published inside the app. A weekly audit reviews the record and writes the corrections back into how the desk works.

What it is not

Editorial analysis and opinion, for information and education. Not financial, investment, legal, security, safety or travel advice. Forecasts are subjective estimates and are often wrong.
