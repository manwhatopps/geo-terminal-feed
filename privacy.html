Parallax — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026.

Parallax ("the app") is published by Jacob Engstrom, an independent developer. It is a read-only news and analysis reader.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, identifiers, usage analytics, or location data. We operate no servers that receive your data.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no user data to manage.
  • What stays on your device. Which articles you have opened and which you have saved are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. They are never transmitted, and they are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Network use. The app makes one kind of network request: fetching its public content feed (JSON files hosted on GitHub). GitHub's servers may log standard request metadata such as an IP address, as any web host does; see GitHub's privacy statement. No request identifies you to us.
  • No third-party SDKs. No advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking frameworks are included. The app does not track you across apps or websites and requests no tracking permission.
  • No permissions. The app does not request location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, or notifications.
  • Children. The app is not directed to children and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Content notice: the app presents geopolitical analysis and probabilistic forecasts as opinion, for informational and educational purposes only. It is not financial, investment, legal, security, safety, or travel advice. See the Content Disclaimer and Terms of Use.
