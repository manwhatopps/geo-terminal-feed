GEO Terminal — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 26, 2026

GEO Terminal is a read-only news and analysis viewer.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, identifiers, usage analytics, or location data.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no user data to manage.
  • Network use. The app makes one kind of network request: fetching its public content feed (a JSON file hosted on GitHub). GitHub's servers may log standard request metadata (such as IP address) as any web host does; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • No third-party SDKs. No advertising, analytics, or tracking frameworks are included.

Content disclaimer: the app presents geopolitical analysis and probabilistic forecasts for informational purposes only. It is not financial, legal, or safety advice.
